NOTES FOR APP REVIEW — GEO Terminal
Bundle ID: com.manwhatopps.geoterminal
Version: 1.0.0

WHAT THE APP DOES
GEO Terminal is a read-only editorial app that presents geopolitical analysis and
probabilistic forecasts written by the developer. It has five tabs: a forecast board,
a world conflict map (Apple Maps with annotated markers), a daily written brief, a
"hypothesis lab," and a weekly long-form lecture. Content is fetched as a static public
JSON feed. There is NO account, NO login, NO user-generated content, NO ads, and NO
data collection.

IS THIS FINANCIAL / SAFETY ADVICE? — NO.
All content is clearly presented as editorial opinion and analysis, not advice. The app
displays a disclaimer on first launch (which the user acknowledges before proceeding) and
links to a full Content Disclaimer, Terms of Service, and Privacy Policy from within the
app. Forecasts are explicitly framed as subjective probabilistic opinion that is often
wrong; the app is not a financial, investment, trading, legal, security, safety, or travel
tool, and states so. No transactions, portfolios, or brokerage features exist.

HOW TO REVIEW
No account or test credentials are needed — nothing is gated. On first launch you will see
a one-time disclaimer; tap "I understand" to enter. Then tap through the five bottom-tab
sections. The map requires no permissions (it does not request location). Pull to refresh
reloads the public content feed.
